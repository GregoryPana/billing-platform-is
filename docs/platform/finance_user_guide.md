# Finance User Guide

This guide focuses only on the finance steps in the billing process. It explains what you review, when you act, and what happens after your approval.

---

## Your role in the process
- You **review and approve** key billing stages.
- Your approval unlocks the next step for billing.
- You **do not** run scripts or notifications.

---

## When you get involved
Finance actions happen at these points:
1. **Move to live** approval (after test runs complete).
2. **Live complete** approval (after live runs complete).
3. **Move to notifications** approval (after post‑live checks, if required by policy).

---

## Step-by-step (Finance)

### Step 1: Review “Move to live” request
**Where:** `Approvals`

What you do:
- Open the request for the billing cycle.
- Read the comments and verify test results are complete.
- Click **Approve** or **Reject**.

What should happen next:
- If approved, billing can generate **live** scripts.
- If rejected, billing must correct issues and resubmit.

---

### Step 2: Review “Live complete” request
**Where:** `Approvals`

What you do:
- Confirm live billing run and printing completed.
- Click **Approve** or **Reject**.

What should happen next:
- If approved, billing can move to notifications.

---

### Step 3: Review “Move to notifications” request (if required)
**Where:** `Approvals`

What you do:
- Confirm post‑live checks are completed.
- Click **Approve** or **Reject**.

What should happen next:
- If approved, billing can generate and run notification commands.

---

## How to decide quickly (non‑technical checklist)
- Are the test or live steps marked **complete** by billing?
- Are there any comments indicating errors or missing data?
- If unsure, **reject with a clear comment** so billing knows what to fix.

---

## Common finance actions
- **Approve** when results are complete and consistent.
- **Reject** when results are missing, unclear, or failed.

---

## If you reject a request
Add a short note like:
- “Test run missing for Cycle X”
- “Live printing not confirmed”
- “Please attach reconciliation details”

Billing will fix and resubmit.
